Run On Earth: Privacy Policy

Published by Samir Studio · Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the game Run On Earth (“the game”), published by Samir Studio (“we”, “us”), handles information.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. The game has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics tools.

3. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, gems, and best distance) and your settings (theme and sound) are saved only on your device. We cannot see them. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, this information is deleted and cannot be restored.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details. Google Play tells the game when a purchase is completed so that the game can add the gems you bought.

5. Third-party services

The game uses Google Play services (Google Play Billing). Google’s own privacy policy applies to them: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Internet connection

The game needs an internet connection to start and to make purchases. We do not collect any data through this connection.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect your personal information, there is nothing for us to keep or delete. To remove the information stored on your device, uninstall the game.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page.
